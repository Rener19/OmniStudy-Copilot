## Design Context

### Users
College/University students who need to read, retain, and review efficiently. The primary job is to transform educational PDFs into an interactive learning engine (via RAG chat, flashcards, and quizzes).

### Brand Personality
Confidence & Focus. The voice and tone should be friendly, clear, and reassuring, helping students feel capable of mastering their subjects.

### Aesthetic Direction
Like Quizlet (friendly, card-based, colorful). While currently utilizing a sleek AI-Native design (Indigo/Emerald with Space Grotesk), the layout should remain card-centric, colorful, and approachable rather than overly austere or "hacker-like." We want a premium tech feel but with Quizlet's inviting, organized warmth.

### Design Principles
1. **Focus First**: Minimize chrome and distractions. Ensure the primary learning content is always center stage.
2. **Friendly & Approachable**: Use soft shadows, rounded corners, and vibrant accents (Indigo/Emerald) to make the heavy AI capabilities feel accessible.
3. **Card-Centric Architecture**: Use physical metaphors like cards and decks (similar to Quizlet) to organize flashcards, quizzes, and chat contexts.
4. **Immediate Feedback**: Use micro-animations (like hover lifts) to give immediate, confident feedback on user interactions without being overwhelming.
